The platform vision

More than another trading agent.

Trading interfaces are becoming easier to automate. Our goal is to make the reasoning, controls, and daily research process more useful—not simply put another chat window in front of a brokerage account.

◈

Market intelligence

Bring market signals, indicators, and research into a repeatable daily workflow.

⌁

Options strategy laboratory

Study defined-risk scenarios, assumptions, liquidity, volatility and tradeoffs before taking action.

▤

Portfolio context

Evaluate ideas against account holdings, available buying power, concentration and risk.

⇄

Brokerage connection layer

Design around supported broker APIs so the strategy experience isn't tied to a single interface.

✓

Approval-first execution

Separate research, simulation and live orders with explicit human review and defined controls.

◷

Daily operating rhythm

Turn one-off analysis into monitored, documented research reports and strategy reviews.

Connected by design

Bring the broker. Keep the intelligence.

Different brokerages have different instruments, data access, execution rules and costs. MoneyDragonApp is being designed to put those differences behind carefully controlled integrations—not pretend every platform behaves the same.

  • Account identity checks before financial data is displayed
  • Provider-specific capabilities and entitlements
  • Clear separation of research and trade execution
  • No API credentials or trading logic in the public website

Development roadmap

Built to earn trust, one step at a time.

This is a product direction, not a claim that all features or brokerage connections are available today.

PHASE 01 · RECOVER

Secure the foundation

Consolidate authentication, restore an intended-account match, verify provider responses and build a dependable read-only connection.

PHASE 02 · RESEARCH

Prove daily usefulness

Deliver recurring market intelligence, options analysis, strategy testing and documented paper-trading results.

PHASE 03 · EXPAND

Earn execution privileges

Evaluate additional integrations and tightly controlled execution only after validation, appropriate permissions and risk review.
